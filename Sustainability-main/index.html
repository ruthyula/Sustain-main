KCP Sustainability

We are committed to sustainability and have set ambitious targets to reduce our carbon footprint and promote a greener future.

Highlands Creamers and Foods (HCF) Ltd

KCB has been at the forefront supporting MSMEs’ transition to sustainable business practices. The Bank has supported Highlands Creamers and Foods (HCF) Ltd in this transition. Commissioned in July 2017, HCF is a family-owned, but independently run dairy and food processing firm based in Nyansiongo, Nyamira County.

Read More

Morara Forest Rehabilitation

KCB in conjunction with Kenya Forestry Organization has been supporting and funding rehabilitation of Morara Forest and sanctuary since 2023. The bank has erected fences and built several pathways to encourage visitors and runners.

At KCB, sustainability is about ensuring long-term business success while contributing towards economic and social development, a healthy environment, and a stable society. We have therefore embraced Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) considerations in our operations and decision-making processes.
